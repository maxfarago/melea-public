1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated as you use melea, and information from third parties.

Account and profile data: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash). You may optionally add a profile photo or other details.

Usage data: We automatically collect information about how you interact with melea, including pages visited, features used, timestamps, and actions taken within the app.

Device and technical data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, and device identifiers.

Communications: If you contact us for support or send us a message, we retain those communications.

2. How we use your data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account and provide the melea service
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Send transactional emails (account verification, password reset, receipts)
  • Send product updates and marketing communications (you may opt out at any time)
  • Improve, personalize, and develop new features
  • Monitor for fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations

Legal basis (GDPR): Depending on the processing activity, we rely on contract performance, legitimate interests, consent, or legal obligation as our lawful basis.

3. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third parties who help us operate melea (e.g., cloud hosting, email delivery, payment processing). They may only use your data as directed by us.
  • Legal requirements: When required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where your data may be transferred to the acquiring entity.
  • With your consent: For any other purpose, with your explicit consent.

4. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate melea, remember your preferences, and understand how you use our service.

  • Essential cookies: Required for the service to function (e.g., session authentication).
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand usage patterns so we can improve the product. On the app we use Google Analytics (see Google's privacy policy).
  • Marketing cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertising (only with your consent).

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may impair app functionality.

5. Payments

melea uses a PCI-DSS-compliant third-party payment processor to handle all payment transactions. We do not store your full card number, CVV, or bank account details on our servers. We retain records of transaction amounts, dates, and billing details for accounting and tax purposes.

Your full payment card details are handled exclusively by our payment processor and are never stored on melea's systems.

6. Location data

We may collect approximate location information derived from your IP address (typically at the city or country level) to provide region-appropriate features, comply with local laws, and prevent fraud.

If melea requests access to your precise device location (GPS), we will ask for your explicit permission beforehand. You can withdraw that permission at any time in your device settings.

7. Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the service. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within [X] days, except where we are required to retain it for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access

Request a copy of the data we hold about you.

Correction

Ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete data.

Deletion

Request erasure of your personal data.

Portability

Receive your data in a machine-readable format.

Objection

Object to certain types of processing.

Restriction

Ask us to limit how we use your data.

Withdraw consent

Revoke consent at any time where processing is consent-based.

Lodge a complaint

File a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@melea.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

EU/UK residents: You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (e.g., your national DPA or the UK ICO).

California residents: Under the CCPA, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information.

9. Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security assessments. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately at security@melea.ai.

10. International data transfers

melea operates globally. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including countries that may have different data protection laws. Where we transfer data from the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK to a third country, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

11. Children

melea is not directed to children under the age of 16 (or 13 in the United States). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.
